Product Requirements Document: Digital Goods Marketplace (MVP)
1. Project Overview
A C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer) and B2C marketplace for instant delivery of digital products (game keys, subscriptions, software licenses).
2. Target Audience
Buyers: Gamers, software seekers, and users looking for regional pricing or specific digital assets.
Sellers: Digital resellers, authorized distributors, and individuals with surplus digital keys.
3. Core MVP Features
A. User Management
Buyer Account: Simple registration (Email/Social) + Purchase history.
Seller Account: Strict verification (KYC/WebMoney Passport integration), rating-based visibility, and sales dashboard.
B. Catalog & Search
Categories: Games, Gift Cards, Software, Accounts.
Filtering: Filter by platform (Steam, Xbox, PSN), region (Global, CIS, US), and price range.
Search: Real-time search with keyword matching.
C. Merchant System (The "Digiseller" Logic)
Product Management: Ability to upload "Unique items" (keys) or "Universal items" (files/links).
Stock Control: Automated tracking of remaining keys.
Instant Delivery: Upon payment confirmation, the system must immediately display the key/file to the user and send an email copy.
D. Transaction & Trust
Multi-Currency Payments: Integration with Stripe, Crypto, and local wallets (WebMoney/Perfect Money).
Escrow/Rating System: Funds are held until the user confirms the item works (or a 72-hour window passes). Ratings are tied to actual sales.
Conflict Resolution: A built-in chat system for buyers and sellers to resolve disputes before admin intervention.
4. Technical Requirements
High Availability: Must handle peak traffic during game launches.
Security: Anti-fraud checks, IP-based purchase restrictions, and data encryption.
SEO Optimization: Dynamic landing pages for trending games (e.g., "Steam Top Up").
